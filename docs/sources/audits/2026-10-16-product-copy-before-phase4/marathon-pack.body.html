Directions

HOW TO USE:

ADAPTOGEN PROTEIN SHAMPOO TRANSFORM YOUR POST-WORKOUT HAIR CARE:

  1. WET HAIR THOROUGHLY
  2. MASSAGE A QUARTER-SIZED AMOUNT INTO SCALP
  3. ALLOW 1-2 MINUTES FOR PROTEIN-RICH FORMULA TO WORK
  4. RINSE COMPLETELY

STRENGTHENING PROTEIN CONDITIONER MAXIMIZE YOUR HAIR'S RECOVERY:

  4. RINSE THOROUGHLY
  5. USE AFTER EVERY WORKOUT FOR BEST RESULTS

COOLING MENTHOL BODY WASH REFRESH AND REVIVE:

  5. FOLLOW WITH MAGNESIUM BODY LOTION

REFUELING MAGNESIUM BODY LOTION RESTORE AND RELAX:
