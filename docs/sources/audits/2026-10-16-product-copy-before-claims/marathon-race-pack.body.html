Anti Chafe Balm — Directions

HOW TO USE:

ENSURE SKIN IS CLEAN AND DRY BEFORE APPLICATION. TWIST UP THE BALM AND APPLY A GENEROUS AMOUNT TO AREAS PRONE TO CHAFING, SUCH AS THIGHS, UNDERARMS, NECK, AND NIPPLES. APPLY THE BODY ANTI CHAFE BALM STRAIGHT FROM THE STICK TO PREVENT THE RUBBING AND FRICTION THAT CAUSES CHAFING, RUB MARKS, AND IRRITATED SKIN. FOR PROLONGED WORKOUTS OR EXPOSURE TO WATER, REAPPLY AS NECESSARY TO MAINTAIN PROTECTION. POST WORKOUT, CLEANSE THE AREA WITH MILD SOAP AND WATER AFTER YOUR WORKOUT TO REMOVE ANY RESIDUE AND MAINTAIN SKIN HEALTH.

STORAGE:

STORE OUR ANTI CHAFE BALM IN A COOL, DRY PLACE, AWAY FROM DIRECT SUNLIGHT FOR OPTIMAL PRESERVATION AND EFFECTIVENESS.

IMPORTANT NOTES:

THIS PRODUCT IS FOR EXTERNAL USE EXCLUSIVELY. AVOID CONTACT WITH EYES. REFRAIN FROM USING ON DAMAGED OR BROKEN SKIN.

FOR BEST RESULTS, APPLY THIS ANTI CHAFE GLIDE BALM AFTER TAKING A BATH WITH OUR COOLING MENTHOL BODY WASH!
