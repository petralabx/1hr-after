# Claims policy

> **Owner:** Class C (human-only)
> **Status:** Scaffold. No claim is approved. Agents must not invent allowed or forbidden claim language beyond the guardrails below.
> **Last updated:** 2026-10-06

## Purpose

Gate for site copy and Meta ads. A sentence is not safe to publish because it appeared on another PLX brand.

## Guardrails that apply before the policy is filled

- No drug, disease, or treatment claims.
- No "clinically proven", "dermatologist tested", or similar proof claims until a human attaches the evidence in [`../sources/`](../sources/) and writes the allowed wording here.
- No competitor name in Meta primary text, headline, or description until this page has a row that allows it.
- Comparative organic copy, if a later row allows it, stays factual and specific. No disparagement.
- Retail prices may appear in organic drafts only after [`../knowledge/products.md`](../knowledge/products.md) lists them. They do not go in Meta ads until this page says so.
- No COGS, fees, or margins anywhere in the repo.
